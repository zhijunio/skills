# PRD outline

Use this default only when the user or repository does not prescribe a format. Omit irrelevant sections or combine them for a bounded capability. Preserve existing conventions and approved history.

```markdown
---
status: draft
approval: pending
version: 0.1
date: YYYY-MM-DD
---

# Product Requirements

## Overview and problem
## Goals and success measures
## Target users and stakeholders
## Current workflows and core scenarios
## Alternatives and supporting evidence
## Target product scope
## Current delivery scope and priorities
## Functional capabilities
## Product quality needs and constraints
## Exclusions and deferred scope
## Dependencies, assumptions and risks
## Open decisions
```

Use actual generation date and the established version scheme. Describe each material capability in terms of purpose, actor or consumer, outcome and scope. Distinguish target-only capabilities from the current delivery scope clearly enough that decomposition cannot mistake them for committed work. Link evidence where available; label proposals and assumptions. Do not fabricate metrics or thresholds. When no baseline or target exists, state the intended measurement and missing decision. Do not duplicate detailed use-case acceptance criteria or technical architecture.

For an approved revision, use the lifecycle instructions in SKILL.md. Preserve the prior approved scope when proposing material changes. A draft is not approved merely because every section is populated.
