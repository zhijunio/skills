# Prototype review

Keep the review proportional to the question.

## Record

- Question and decision owner.
- Source product or behavior scope, assumptions and excluded questions.
- Artifact path, mode, alternatives, fixtures and reproducible review steps.
- Expected success criteria and actual observations; distinguish agent checks from human feedback.
- Accepted, rejected and unresolved proposals with rationale and affected documents.
- Limitations, checks not run and remaining risks.

Use the existing decision record when available; otherwise report in the requested format. A prototype awaiting human review is not approved. Simulated behavior is not proof of deployed behavior. Do not invent participant counts, task success rates or numerical measurements.

## Route feedback

Product scope and journey changes go to the PRD owner. Business rule changes go to the behavior specification or domain owner. Technical unknowns go to system design or a scoped feasibility experiment. Material changes require approval through the applicable lifecycle. Do not automatically change the use-case index, implementation status or production code.

Record retention or cleanup needs. No remote branch, publication, issue mutation or deployment is implied by prototype review. Any later implementation must meet the project's normal quality requirements.
